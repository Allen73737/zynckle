1. Navigate to your repository: [https://github.com/Allen73737/zynckle](https://github.com/Allen73737/zynckle)
2. At the top right of the code tab, locate the **About** section and click the **⚙️ (Settings gear icon)**.
3. Paste the **Description** above.
4. Set the **Website** URL.
5. In the **Topics** field, type or paste the 20 topics listed above.
6. Check **Releases**, **Packages**, and **Environments**.
7. Click **Save changes**.
